Spotly helps families find genuinely kid-friendly places, plan the day with AI, and keep the memories. We're in closed beta on Google Play — join in, try it out, and your feedback steers what we build next. Takes about 5 minutes.

① Join the testers group → ② Become a tester on Google Play →

Do both steps on your Android phone, signed in with the Google account you’ll use. Right after step ①, step ② may say “not available” for a few minutes — that’s Google syncing; just reopen it shortly.

How to join — 4 quick steps

1
Join the testers group

Tap “① Join the testers group” above and join (you may need to tap “Join group”). This is what gives your Google account access to the test.

2
Become a tester

Tap “② Become a tester” and accept. If it says “App not available”, wait a few minutes after joining the group and reopen it — Google is still syncing.

3
Install Spotly from Google Play

You’ll get a “Download it on Google Play” link. Install it — it may show “(Unreleased)”, which is normal for a test.

4
Try it & keep it ~2 weeks

Create an account, plan a day out or save a place, and please leave it installed for 14 days — open it now and then. That’s what lets Google approve the app. 🙏

What you need

✓
An Android phone (this beta is Android-only for now).
✓
A Google account — the same Gmail you use on that phone.
✓
Keep Spotly installed for 14 days and open it occasionally.
Stuck on “App not available”? Make sure you joined the testers group (step ①) with the same Google account, then wait a few minutes and reopen step ②. Still stuck after a day? Email contact@meetspotly.com with your Gmail and we’ll sort it.

Found a bug or have an idea?

We read every message. Tell us what to fix or add — it genuinely shapes the app.
