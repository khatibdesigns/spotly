Spotly helps families find genuinely kid-friendly places, plan the day with AI, and keep the memories. We're in closed beta on Google Play — join in, try it out, and your feedback steers what we build next. Takes about 5 minutes.

Become a tester on Google Play →

Open this on your Android phone, signed in with the Google account you’ll use.

How to join — 3 steps

1
Tap “Become a tester”

On your Android phone (signed into your Google account), tap the button above and accept the invite.

2
Install Spotly from Google Play

You’ll get a “Download it on Google Play” link. Install it — it may show “(Unreleased)”, which is normal for a test.

3
Try it & keep it for ~2 weeks

Create an account, plan a day out or save a place, and please leave it installed for 14 days — open it now and then. That’s what lets Google approve the app. 🙏

What you need

✓
An Android phone (this beta is Android-only for now).
✓
A Google account — the same Gmail you use on that phone.
✓
Keep Spotly installed for 14 days and open it occasionally.
If the button says “not a tester” or the Play page won’t load, your account may not be on the list yet, or Google is still publishing. Email support@meetspotly.com with your Gmail and we’ll add you within a day.
