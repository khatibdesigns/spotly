# 🎥 Spotly Teaser 01 — Shot Prompts (Higgsfield · Veo 3 · Kling)

Generate **only shots 1, 4, 5, 6, 7**. Shots **3 (app)** and **8 (pin-map)** are already finished in `Spotly-Teaser01-EN/AR.mp4`. Each clip: **9:16, ~5s**, then trim to ~2s in the edit.

> **Tool pick:** Veo 3 (best for realistic light + gentle motion) → Kling 2.x (accessible alt) → Higgsfield (only if you want a dramatic camera move). Higgsfield is **image→video**, so you make a still first, then apply a camera preset.

**Global negative (Kling field; bake into prompt for Veo/Higgsfield):**
> `close-up faces, distorted or melted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, on-screen text, watermark, logo, cartoon, cgi look, oversaturated, fast or chaotic motion`

**The one rule:** never a clear child's face — only **backs, hands, silhouettes, or motion-blur**.

## SHOT 1 — Dread (0–2s)
**🖼️ Image / Veo prompt:** A six-year-old child sitting **from behind** on a beige sofa in a sun-drenched Kuwaiti living room, slumped and bored. Harsh warm afternoon light blasts through sheer white curtains; dust motes drift; date palms outside the window. No face visible. *Shot on 35mm, warm golden-hour grade, soft light, shallow depth of field, film grain, coral-and-cream palette, photorealistic, vertical 9:16.*
- **Higgsfield:** preset **Dolly In** (or *Zoom In*), strength **Low/Slow** · motion: "slow push toward the child; curtains drift."
- **Veo 3 / Kling add:** "camera slowly pushes in; sheer curtains sway gently; dust motes float; no audio."

## SHOT 4 — Cool morning park (7–9s)
**🖼️ Image / Veo prompt:** Wide cinematic view of a lush green Gulf park at **golden morning hour**, tall date palms, dewy grass, soft sun flare. A family is **small and distant**, walking away from camera toward a colorful playground. *Shot on 35mm, warm grade, soft light, shallow DOF, film grain, coral-and-cream palette, photorealistic, vertical 9:16.*
- **Higgsfield:** preset **Crane Up** or **Move Right**, slow · motion: "gentle slow pan right; leaves sway."
- **Veo 3 / Kling add:** "slow gentle pan right; warm lens flare blooms; leaves move softly."

## SHOT 5 — Midday indoor play (9–11s)
**🖼️ Image / Veo prompt:** Interior of a **bright modern indoor children's soft-play** area — colorful foam shapes, a ball pit, large windows with soft daylight. A cheerful **blur of children playing**, faces indistinct from motion blur, clean contemporary design. *Warm grade, soft light, shallow DOF, film grain, photorealistic, vertical 9:16.*
- **Higgsfield:** preset **Handheld**, subtle · motion: "subtle handheld drift; kids blur with motion."
- **Veo 3 / Kling add:** "subtle handheld camera; children play in happy motion blur."

## SHOT 6 — Ice-cream / sunset joy (11–13s)
**🖼️ Image / Veo prompt:** Extreme close-up of **two small children's hands** holding melting ice-cream cones, drips running down. Warm **coral sunset bokeh** behind, golden backlight, candid and joyful. No faces. *Shot on 35mm, slow-motion feel, shallow focus, film grain, photorealistic, vertical 9:16.*
- **Higgsfield:** preset **Dolly In** (gentle) / **Macro** · motion: "slow push on the hands; ice cream drips; bokeh shimmers."
- **Veo 3 / Kling add:** "slow motion; gentle push-in on the hands; ice cream slowly drips; warm bokeh shimmer."

## SHOT 7 — Seafront silhouettes (13–15s)
**🖼️ Image / Veo prompt:** **Silhouettes** of a family — two adults and two children — walking along a calm **Gulf seafront at golden sunset**, fully backlit, warm sky, gentle waves, reflections on wet sand. Pure silhouettes, no facial detail. *Cinematic, warm grade, film grain, lens flare, photorealistic, vertical 9:16.*
- **Higgsfield:** preset **Move Left** / **Super Dolly**, slow · motion: "camera tracks alongside the silhouettes; waves roll gently."
- **Veo 3 / Kling add:** "camera slowly tracks alongside the walking silhouettes; gentle waves; sunset shimmer."
