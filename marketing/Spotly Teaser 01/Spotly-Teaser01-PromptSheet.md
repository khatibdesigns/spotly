# 🤖 Spotly Teaser 01 — AI Video Prompt Sheet (Veo / Kling / Higgsfield)

Generate shots **1, 4, 5, 6, 7** with AI. Shots **3** (app screen + itinerary) and **8** (map + pins) are **motion graphics**, not AI — use the provided animatic or rebuild in CapCut. Generate each clip at **9:16**, 4–6s, then trim to the beat in edit.

---

## 🎨 PASTE INTO EVERY PROMPT (style + negatives)

**Style suffix (append to each prompt):**
> `cinematic, warm golden-hour color grade, soft natural light, shallow depth of field, 35mm film look, gentle film grain, coral and cream palette, authentic Gulf / Kuwait family lifestyle, candid documentary feel, 9:16 vertical, hyperrealistic, subtle slow camera movement`

**Negative prompt (Kling/Runway field; for Veo write it into the prompt):**
> `no close-up faces, no distorted or melted faces, no extra fingers, no deformed hands, no on-screen text, no watermark, no logos, not cartoonish, no cgi look, no fast or chaotic motion, no uncanny eyes`

**Golden rule:** keep people as **backs / hands / silhouettes / motion-blur** — never a clear child's face.

## 🎬 SHOT 1 — Dread (0–2s)
> A six-year-old child seen **from behind**, slumped and bored on a beige sofa in a sun-drenched Kuwaiti living room. Harsh warm 40°C afternoon light blasts through sheer white curtains; dust motes drift in the light; date palms visible through the window. Slow, gentle push-in. — *[style suffix]*

- **Kling:** Professional mode, 5s, camera "zoom in" slow.
- **Higgsfield:** generate/keep a still of the scene → apply **"Push-In (slow)"** camera preset.
- **Veo 3:** add "no audio, slow dolly in."

## 🎬 SHOT 4 — Cool morning park (7–9s)
> Wide cinematic shot of a lush green park in the Gulf at golden morning hour. Tall date palms, dewy grass, soft sun flare. A family is **small in the frame**, walking away from camera toward a colorful playground. Slow gentle pan right. — *[style suffix]*

- **Higgsfield:** camera preset **"Crane Up"** or **"Slow Pan."**
- **Kling:** add a light "lens flare," motion strength low.

## 🎬 SHOT 5 — Midday indoor play (9–11s)
> Interior of a bright, modern indoor children's soft-play area — colorful foam shapes, a ball pit, clean contemporary design, soft daylight through large windows. A cheerful **blur of kids playing**, faces indistinct from motion blur. Subtle handheld drift. — *[style suffix]*

- **Kling/Runway:** increase motion strength slightly for the play blur.
- **Higgsfield:** preset **"Handheld (subtle)."**

## 🎬 SHOT 6 — Ice-cream / sunset joy (11–13s)
> Extreme close-up of **two small children's hands** holding melting ice-cream cones, drips running down. Warm coral sunset bokeh behind them, golden backlight. Slow motion, candid and joyful. No faces in frame. — *[style suffix]*

- **Higgsfield:** preset **"Macro / slow push."**

## 🎬 SHOT 7 — Seafront silhouettes (13–15s)
> Cinematic shot of the **silhouettes** of a family — two adults and two children — walking along a calm Gulf seafront at golden sunset. Fully backlit, warm sky, gentle waves, soft reflections on wet sand. Slow tracking shot alongside them. Pure silhouettes, no facial detail. — *[style suffix]*

- **Higgsfield:** preset **"Tracking / Dolly side."**

## ⚙️ Tool cheatsheet
| Tool | Best for | Note |
|------|----------|------|
| **Veo 3 / Veo 2** | realistic people + light, native audio off | strongest realism; describe motion in words |
| **Kling 2.0** | cinematic motion, control | use **Professional mode**, 5s, negative-prompt field |
| **Higgsfield** | dramatic **camera moves** | image→video: make a still, then apply a camera preset (Push-In, Crane, Tracking, Macro) |
| Runway Gen-4 / Pika 2 | backups | fine for b-roll if the above are busy |

**Workflow:** generate 2–3 takes per shot → pick the best → trim to ~2s → assemble per the package → one final color grade.
