## Audio layering (this is what makes the montage cohere)
- **A1 — Music bed:** one track, full length, the spine. EN = warm uplifting indie; AR = same energy + a light oud/qanun layer. (Artlist / Epidemic / Uppbeat — search "warm uplifting family hopeful".)
- **A2 — Voiceover:** the 4 lines, sitting on top; **duck the music ~4–5 dB** under the VO. Record a warm Kuwaiti voice for AR, soft natural read for EN (or ElevenLabs multilingual v2).
- **A3 — Veo ambience:** keep each clip's own sound but **low (−18 to −24 dB)** for texture. **Mute** any Veo clip that generated stray speech or music.
- **A4 — SFX:** a clean **ding** at 4.5s (the tap) and soft **pops** under the pins at 15.2–17s.

## Text (CapCut) settings
- Font: **Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold** (EN) / a clean bold Arabic like **Cairo/Tajawal** (AR). Color: white on the b-roll shots, with a soft shadow. Keep all text inside the **middle 80%** (away from Reels/TikTok/Snap UI).
- Or just import the provided `.srt` as soft captions.
