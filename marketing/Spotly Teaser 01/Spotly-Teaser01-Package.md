# 🎬 Spotly · Teaser 01 — "خلّها تستاهل / Make Them Count"

**Concept ① (emotional + filling-map pin device) · AI-generated · Kuwaiti-Khaleeji + English · 9:16 vertical · ~18s**

---

## The strategy in one line
A first teaser makes people *feel*, plants one brand device (the filling map of coral pins), and drives one action (join the beta / "coming soon"). We tease the feeling — we don't demo features.

## The craft rule for AI video (read this first)
AI still botches **kids' faces**. So we **never show them**. Every shot uses **backs, hands, silhouettes, or motion-blur**, plus our own **map+pins motion graphics** and a **real app screen-recording** for product beats. Benefits: premium look, zero uncanny valley, and **no character-consistency problem** (each shot is a different moment, so the model never has to "remember" a face).

## Voiceover scripts (record two separate cuts)

**English** — warm, intimate, unhurried:
> "Another weekend already… One tap, and the whole day's planned — for your kids, and the weather. Every outing, a memory you keep. **Spotly. Coming soon.**"

**Khaleeji (Kuwaiti)** — warm; *transliteration for the voice actor / Arabic TTS*:
> «ويكند ثاني… ووين نوديهم؟» — *wēkend thāni… o wēn nwaddīhum?*
> «بضغطة وحدة، يومكم كله مرتّب — على مزاج العيال وعلى الجو.» — *bi-ḏ̣aghṭa waḥda, yōmkum killa mrattab — ʿala mzāj il-ʿyāl w ʿala il-jaww.*
> «كل طلعة… ذكرى تبقى.» — *kil ṭalʿa… thikra tibga.*
> «**Spotly… قريبًا.**» — *Spotly… gariiban.*

**VO production:** record a real Kuwaiti voice if you can. Fast alternative: **ElevenLabs** (multilingual v2) — pick a warm voice, paste the Arabic; for English a soft natural read. Keep both unrushed.

## Look & sound
- **Color:** warm golden-hour, coral (#fa7959) + cream (#fcfaf6), soft contrast, gentle film grain.
- **Lens:** 35mm feel, shallow depth of field, subtle slow camera motion only.
- **Music — EN cut:** warm uplifting indie (soft piano + claps), builds at the map.
- **Music — AR cut:** same build with a light **oud/qanun** motif so it feels native.
- **SFX:** a satisfying *ding* on the tap (shot 3) and a soft *pop* on each pin drop (shot 8).
- **Music sources:** Artlist / Epidemic Sound / Uppbeat (free). Search "warm uplifting family" + an "oud" layer for the AR cut.

## Assembly (CapCut / Premiere)
1. Project: **1080×1920, 18s, 30fps.**
2. Lay the AI clips (shots 1,4,5,6,7) in order; trim each to its beat (~2s).
3. Drop in the **app screen-recording + animated itinerary** at shot 3 and the **pin-map** at shot 8 (use the motion-graphics animatic provided, or rebuild in CapCut).
4. Add the **VO** track, then **music**, then **SFX** (tap + pin pops).
5. On-screen text in the safe zone — keep text within the middle 80% (away from TikTok/Reels/Snap UI on edges).
6. Burn in captions from the `.srt` files (provided) or keep them as soft subs.
7. Final unified **color grade** so AI clips + graphics match.
8. Export masters: **AR** + **EN**, plus a **6s bumper** (shots 3→8→logo) and a **16:9** site cut.
