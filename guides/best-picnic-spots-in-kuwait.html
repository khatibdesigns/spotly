Best picnic spots in Kuwait

Updated 2026 · A parent-tested guide by Spotly

A blanket, a cool box and a bit of green is one of the easiest, cheapest family days out there is. Kuwait has landscaped parks, seafront lawns and — in spring — an open desert that turns green, all perfect for a picnic. Here are ten of the best picnic spots for families, and how to time it so everyone's comfortable.

The best picnic spots, one by one

Hours and entry fees change with the season, so check current details in the Spotly app before you head out.

1. Al Shaheed Park lawns — Kuwait City

Wide, well-kept lawns and gardens right in the city, with shaded paths and plenty of room to spread out. A relaxed, central pick for a morning or a cool evening picnic.

2. Green Island (Al Jazeera Al Khadra) — Gulf Road

Grassy areas with sea views all around and a small children's play area to keep the kids busy between snacks. There's a small entry fee, and the breeze makes it feel cooler than inland.

3. The Marina & Salmiya seafront

Grass and benches along the Gulf Road with a steady sea breeze — lovely at sunset, and you're never far from an ice-cream stop when the picnic runs dry.

4. Messila Beach

A calmer stretch of seafront with room to lay a blanket, let the kids paddle at the edge and watch the boats go by. Great for a low-key family afternoon.

5. Al Kout Waterfront — Fahaheel

Open walkways and green corners beside the fountains, handy if you want a picnic that can turn into dinner or dessert at the mall next door.

6. The Boulevard lakeside — Salmiya

Landscaped lawns around a lake with shade, cafés and flat paths for strollers. An easy, pretty spot for a picnic with a coffee run built in.

7. Al Shaab Leisure Park — Salmiya

Green space and walkways along the seafront that work well for a spread-out family picnic on a cooler day.

8. The green desert (spring) — Kabd & the north

After the winter rains, Kuwait's desert turns green and families head out for classic spring picnics and camping. Go with a group, pack shade and water, and check the forecast — this is a seasonal treat, best from late winter into spring.

9. Entertainment City grounds — Doha

Beyond the rides there's open outdoor space to picnic before or after a visit, making a full family day of it.

10. Your neighbourhood public garden

Almost every area has a public garden with grass, shade and a playground — the zero-effort, close-to-home picnic. Spotly can find the nearest one to you.

What to pack for a Kuwait picnic

  • Shade — a pop-up tent or umbrella. Even in cooler months the sun is strong.
  • A cool box & plenty of water — more than you think you'll need.
  • A wipeable mat — grass can be watered or dusty depending on the spot.
  • Sunscreen, hats and a small first-aid kit — the family-outing essentials.
  • A ball or scooter — somewhere for the kids to burn energy between snacks.

Frequently asked questions

Where are the best picnic spots in Kuwait?
Family favourites include the lawns at Al Shaheed Park, Green Island, the Marina and Salmiya seafront, Messila Beach, the Al Kout waterfront, the Boulevard lakeside, and — in spring — the green desert around Kabd and the north.
When is the best time for a picnic in Kuwait?
Roughly November to April for comfortable daytime picnics. In warmer months, go early morning or after sunset and pick a shaded or breezy spot. Spring is the classic season for a desert picnic.
Are there free picnic spots in Kuwait?
Yes — most public parks, gardens and seafront areas are free. A few attractions like Green Island charge a small entry fee. Check current hours and fees in the Spotly app before you go.
