Things to do in Kuwait this weekend with kids

Updated 2026 · A parent-tested guide by Spotly

Friday morning rolls around and the kids are already asking where you're going. Instead of scrambling, plan the weekend around one simple rhythm that works almost every week in Kuwait: cool morning outdoors, air-conditioned midday, seafront evening. Here's how to make Thursday to Saturday easy.

The weekend rhythm that always works

The secret to a great Kuwait weekend isn't a single attraction — it's timing. The heat decides when you go out, not just where. Follow this three-part rhythm and you'll never melt in a midday car park again:

  • Morning (outdoors): start early while it's cool — a park, a playground or a stroll by the water before the sun climbs.
  • Midday (indoors, AC): the hottest hours are for air conditioning — an indoor amusement park, soft play, the aquarium, a museum, or a long family lunch.
  • Evening (seafront): when the sun drops, head to the Gulf Road or the marina for a sea breeze, scooters and an early dinner.

Thursday: an easy start

The weekend often opens with everyone a little tired from the week, so keep Thursday low-effort. An evening outing suits it best: a relaxed seafront walk along the Gulf Road, ice cream and a bit of running-around to burn off energy before bedtime. If the little ones are restless earlier, the big malls give you indoor play and dinner under one roof.

Friday: the main event

Friday is your big day, so use all three parts of the rhythm. Get out early to a green park or playground while it's still pleasant. As the heat builds toward midday, move indoors — an indoor amusement park, soft play zone, the aquarium or a science and cultural centre keeps everyone happy through the worst of the sun. Then, once it cools, finish at the seafront with a sunset walk and an early family dinner.

Saturday: recharge before the week

Saturday is for something calmer before school and work return. A museum or cultural centre is an easy win — interesting for older kids, cool and unhurried for everyone. Pair it with a family lunch nearby, and if the evening is mild, a short outdoor stop by the water rounds things off without overdoing it.

Frequently asked questions

What can families do in Kuwait this weekend?
Build the weekend around the weather: an outdoor morning at a park or the seafront, an air-conditioned midday at an indoor amusement park, soft play, the aquarium or a museum, then a sunset walk along the Gulf Road and an early dinner.
What is there to do in Kuwait on a hot weekend?
Keep midday indoors and air-conditioned — indoor amusement parks and soft play in the big malls, the aquarium, science and cultural centres, the cinema, or a long lunch with a play area — and save parks and the seafront for early morning or after sunset.
What time should we go out with kids in Kuwait?
In warm months, go outdoors early or after sunset and stay indoors through midday. From about November to March the weather is pleasant for most of the day.
