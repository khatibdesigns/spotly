Things to do in Salmiya with kids

Updated 2026 · A parent-tested guide by Spotly

Salmiya is one of the easiest neighbourhoods in Kuwait for a family day out. It's compact and walkable, packed with malls, family restaurants and indoor entertainment, and it sits right on the Gulf Road seafront. That mix means you can stay cool indoors through the heat and step out for a breeze at sunset — all within a short distance.

Malls & indoor play

Salmiya's malls are a family staple, and not just for shopping. Most have an indoor play area or arcade, a cinema, and a wide choice of family restaurants and cafés under one air-conditioned roof. For little ones, a soft-play zone or a few rounds at the arcade can fill an hour or two happily, while older kids gravitate to the games and the screens. The big advantage here is convenience: parking, clean facilities, food and play are all in one place, so nobody has to step into the heat between activities.

The science & aquarium area

One of Salmiya's signature draws for families is its science and aquarium attractions near the waterfront. Kids love watching marine life up close, and the hands-on, interactive science exhibits turn a visit into something that feels more like play than a lesson. It's a great anchor for a morning or early afternoon — somewhere cool, engaging and genuinely educational. Check current opening days and ticketing before you set out, since these can vary by season.

The seafront & Gulf Road

When the sun drops, the Gulf Road seafront in Salmiya comes alive. Long, flat promenades are perfect for scooters, balance bikes and strollers, there's a steady sea breeze, and plenty of spots to stop for ice cream. A sunset walk along the water is the kind of simple, low-cost outing kids remember — and it pairs beautifully with an early dinner nearby.

Family dining

Salmiya is full of family-friendly restaurants and cafés, from quick casual spots to sit-down places with high chairs and kids' menus. The density is the win: you're rarely more than a few minutes from somewhere that works for the whole family. Look for places with a little play corner or open space if you've got toddlers who can't sit still for long.

A compact half-day plan

  • Late morning: start indoors — the science and aquarium attractions, or a mall play area while it's hot outside.
  • Lunch: a family restaurant nearby, ideally with a play corner for the little ones.
  • Afternoon rest: a quiet café or some browsing while energy recharges.
  • Sunset: head to the Gulf Road seafront for a walk, scooters and ice cream.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do in Salmiya with young kids?
Malls with indoor play areas and arcades, the science and aquarium attractions, family restaurants, and the Gulf Road seafront for an evening stroll — all in a small, walkable area.
Is Salmiya good for a family day out when it's hot?
Yes. Much of the family activity is indoors and air-conditioned, so you can stay cool through midday and save the seafront for after sunset.
