The Avenues with kids

Updated 2026 · A parent-tested guide by Spotly

When the heat outside makes a park or beach off-limits, Kuwait's biggest mall becomes one of the best places to spend a whole day with the family. The Avenues isn't just shopping — it's indoor play, an arcade, a cinema and dozens of family restaurants under one air-conditioned roof, all laid out across distinct districts you can wander between. Here's how to turn it into a smooth, beat-the-heat family day.

Why it works for families

The big draw is everything in one place. Parking, clean facilities, food, play and entertainment all sit under the same roof, so once you're in, nobody has to step back into the heat. For parents, that means fewer transitions, fewer meltdowns, and a day that flows from one stop to the next without a single drive in between.

Indoor play & the arcade

This is usually where a family day at the mall begins. Indoor play areas give younger kids room to climb, slide and burn energy, while arcades pull in older children and tweens with games and prizes. An hour or two here early in the day takes the edge off restless energy and sets everyone up for a calmer lunch.

Cinema time

A film is a perfect mid-day reset. After a busy morning of play, an hour or two in a cool, dark cinema lets younger kids settle and gives parents a breather. Check showtimes ahead so you can build the rest of the day around a screening that suits nap schedules and bedtimes.

Family dining

The choice of family restaurants is one of the mall's biggest strengths. From quick casual bites to sit-down meals with high chairs and kids' menus, there's something for every age and mood. Tip: eat a little early to beat the crowds, and pick somewhere near where you'll head next to keep the walking short for little legs.

Explore the districts

Part of what makes The Avenues feel different from a standard mall is its districts — each with its own look and feel. A slow walk through them is an activity in itself: kids enjoy the changing scenery, the open spaces and the people-watching, and it's a gentle way to wind down between bigger stops.

A beat-the-heat all-day plan

  • Late morning: arrive and head straight to the indoor play area or arcade to burn energy.
  • Lunch: a family restaurant with a kids' menu, eaten a touch early to avoid the rush.
  • Early afternoon: a cinema showing — a cool, calm reset for everyone.
  • Late afternoon: a relaxed stroll through the districts, a snack and a little browsing before home.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Avenues good for a family day out with kids?
Yes — as Kuwait's biggest mall it combines indoor play, an arcade, a cinema and dozens of family restaurants under one air-conditioned roof, making it a strong all-day, beat-the-heat option.
How long can you spend at The Avenues with children?
