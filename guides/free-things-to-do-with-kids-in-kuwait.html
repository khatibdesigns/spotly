Free things to do with kids in Kuwait

Updated 2026 · A parent-tested guide by Spotly

A great family day doesn't have to cost a thing. Some of the best memories come from a park, a beach or a sunset walk. Here are the free and nearly-free ways to spend a day with the kids in Kuwait.

Parks & gardens

Public parks are the easiest free outing: green space to run, a playground, and shade for a snack break. Go early morning or after sunset in the warm months. Pack a picnic and you've got a half-day plan for nothing.

The seafront & the Marina

The Gulf Road and Marina promenades are free, flat and lovely — perfect for scooters, balance bikes and strollers, with a sea breeze and ice-cream stops along the way. Sunset is the magic hour.

The beach

Public beaches are a classic low-cost day. Bucket, spade, a bit of shade and water — toddlers and big kids alike will happily lose an hour to the sand. Avoid the midday sun and bring plenty of water.

Mall play areas & window adventures

On a hot day, the larger malls offer free, air-conditioned play areas and plenty to look at. It's a no-cost way to stay cool and let the kids stretch their legs.

Free cultural days

Keep an eye out for free or low-cost family days at cultural centres and community events — a great way to mix things up without spending much.

Frequently asked questions

What are free things to do with kids in Kuwait?
Parks and gardens, the Gulf Road and Marina seafront, public beaches, and air-conditioned play areas inside larger malls. Many cultural centres also have free family days.
Where can families walk for free in Kuwait?
The Gulf Road and Marina promenades — free, flat, and great for scooters, bikes and strollers, best at early morning or sunset.
