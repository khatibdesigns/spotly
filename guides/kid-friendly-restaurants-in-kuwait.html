Kid-friendly restaurants in Kuwait

Updated 2026 · A parent-tested guide by Spotly

Eating out with young kids is a different sport. The right restaurant turns a stressful meal into the best part of the day; the wrong one ends in a toddler under the table. Here's what to look for in Kuwait — and how to find it fast.

What makes a restaurant genuinely kid-friendly

  • A play area or eat-and-play setup — the holy grail. Kids eat, then burn off energy steps from your table.
  • High chairs and a kids' menu — so you're not splitting an adult plate three ways.
  • Changing facilities and stroller space — small things that make or break an outing with a baby.
  • Forgiving acoustics and a relaxed crowd — somewhere a bit of noise won't earn you side-eye.
  • Halal options — easy to find across Kuwait, but worth confirming for specific cuisines.

Where to find them

Inside and around the malls

The big malls are reliable for family dining: clusters of restaurants with kids' menus, plus an indoor play centre or arcade nearby so you can pair lunch with an hour of fun without going back into the heat. This is the easiest "eat + play" combo in the country.

The waterfront & Marina

For cooler days and evenings, the Marina and seafront dining areas are lovely — open space for a stroller, a sea breeze, and somewhere to walk it off after.

Salmiya & Hawally

Dense with casual family spots and cafés. Good for a quick, low-key meal between activities.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a restaurant kid-friendly in Kuwait?
High chairs, a kids' menu, changing facilities, stroller space, and ideally a play area. Many family restaurants in the malls and along the waterfront have these.
Which restaurants in Kuwait have play areas?
Many family restaurants inside or near the larger malls pair dining with a soft-play or arcade, so kids can eat then play in the same place.
