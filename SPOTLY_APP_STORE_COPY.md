## Description (iOS 4000 char max / Play "Full description" 4000 char max)

**Never wonder "where should we take the kids?" again.**

Spotly is the family app for discovering great places to go — this weekend, this afternoon, anywhere in the world — and for keeping the memories you make there.

Tired of the same three spots every weekend? Spotly curates kid-friendly places around you: restaurants and cafés with play areas, parks, indoor playgrounds, farms, museums, beaches and more — filtered for your kids' ages and what matters to you.

**FIND THE RIGHT SPOT**
• A handpicked "This week near you" list, tuned to your location, your kids' ages, and the season
• Filter by play area, indoor/outdoor, free, food on site, stroller-friendly, open now and more
• See real amenities at a glance: play areas, changing rooms, parking, age suitability
• Book a table or reserve your spot right inside the app, where available

**PLAN THE DAY**
• Build a simple plan, add a few spots, get a reminder before you go
• A friendly check-in afterward ("How was it?") saves the day to your history

**KEEP THE MEMORIES**
• Add your family photos to every place you visit
• Watch your personal world map fill up with pins — Kuwait, Europe, the USA, everywhere you've been
• Build a beautiful family history you can scroll back through for years

**TURN MEMORIES INTO KEEPSAKES**
• Order custom printed photo albums of your trips — "Our France Trip" — and prints, delivered to your door

**SPOTLY PLUS**
Upgrade for unlimited photo storage, your full memory map, personalized weekly picks, all filters, and unlimited saves and plans. Free to start; subscribe anytime.

Spotly — all about the family.
