The idea

One place for the whole family weekend

Parents waste their limited free time deciding where to go — and end up repeating the same three places. Generic maps surface bars, cafés and shisha lounges; they don't understand a 3-year-old, a halal preference, or 45°C heat at 3 PM. Spotly is built around the family from the first tap.

The problem
  • "Where do we take the kids this weekend?" — decision fatigue, every week.
  • Map apps aren't curated for children, ages, dietary needs or weather.
  • Outing photos get lost in a 10,000-photo camera roll.
  • Local family venues struggle to reach the right parents.
The Spotly answer
  • Every place is AI-screened for genuine kid-friendliness before it's shown.
  • Recommendations adapt to your kids' ages, food preferences & the live weather.
  • A built-in memory map & gallery that becomes printed keepsake albums.
  • A merchant side + CRM so venues list, sell and measure — building the network.

For families

Discover & the AI Trip Planner

Discover

AI-screened, family-first feed

Live results from Google Places are each judged once by Claude AI for genuine kid-friendliness (parks, play areas, museums, family dining) — filtering out bars, cafés & shisha lounges. Verdicts are cached globally so the catalogue keeps getting smarter.

Discover

Smart filters & context

Halal, hotels & resorts, indoor / outdoor, waterparks, price, open-now, "tastes they'll love" dining. A live weather chip, a location picker, "search this area", photo carousels, and an instant-loading feed.

AI Planner

Plain-language day plans

"4 days in Paris with two kids, ~€150/day." Spotly returns a sequenced, age-appropriate, multi-day itinerary with real places, photos, map pins, time slots and estimated costs — saved straight into Plans & your calendar.

AI Planner · new

Weather-aware & editable

The planner now reads the destination's live weather and avoids outdoor stops during peak heat. Swap any stop for another of the same category with one tap (↻), add or remove stops and days, and get a push notification the moment your plan is ready — even if the app is closed.

What makes the planner different

Knows your family

Pulls kids' ages, favourite foods and allergies from the profile to bias and exclude suggestions.

Runs in the cloud

Generation runs on a server job — close the app and a notification brings you back to the finished plan.

Real, bookable places

Every stop is a real venue with coordinates, photos and a path to booking or directions.

Memory Map & Gallery → Printed Albums

Add a photo + place after each outing; Spotly pins it to your private world map and builds a visited "passport". When the trip's done, turn the gallery into a beautiful custom printed album ("Our France Trip") — physical keepsakes shipped to your door, the long-term growth engine.

For families

Family, bookings & commerce

Family

Shared family account

Multiple adults under one family via deep-link invites (meetspotly.com/join). Each parent appears as themselves; kids' profiles drive recommendations. Family-wide plans, memories and saves.

Bookings

Request-to-book + QR pass

Reserve a place in-app → get a branded confirmation email and a QR pass. Merchants scan to redeem. Add to calendar in one tap.

Commerce

Prepaid vouchers & cart

Venues sell prepaid offers (e.g. "pay 10 → get 15 balance"). A global cart spans places, multi-currency (KWD, SAR, AED, USD, EUR…), with a QR voucher pass and "My Purchases".

Commerce

Scan-to-redeem

Booking & voucher QR codes are redeemed by the merchant via in-app camera or the web CRM (camera or USB scanner) — closing the loop from discovery to paid visit.

Built global from day one

Arabic & RTL

Full English + Arabic localisation with right-to-left layout; GCC-first, internationally ready.

Multi-currency

Gulf & Western currencies with correct symbols and decimals throughout commerce.

Push & analytics

Firebase Cloud Messaging campaigns + GA4 analytics wired across both platforms.

For venues

The merchant side & CRM

Spotly isn't just a directory — it's a two-sided platform. Family venues claim their listing, sell offers, take bookings and measure performance, both inside the app and through a web CRM at meetspotly.com/partners.

Hierarchy

Brands, countries & branches

A four-tier model — Super-admin → Brand owner → Country manager → Branch manager — with permissions enforced at the database level. A branch manager literally cannot see another branch's revenue. Managers onboard by invite or self-claim with owner approval.

Analytics

Insights, trends & forecasts

Tap any metric — views, clicks, bookings, vouchers, revenue, clients — for a 30-day trend chart, a 7-day forecast, a per-branch breakdown and the underlying customer list.

Growth

Promoted placement

Merchants can request promotion: a paid placement floats them to the top of Discover with a badge and a highlighted map pin.

Control

Offers & live toggle

Manage vouchers and currency, take a branch offline/online instantly, and redeem QR passes — all role-scoped.

A self-curating catalogue

Two supply sources reinforce each other: AI screening builds a vetted base of real venues automatically, while merchants opt in to enrich their own listings with offers, photos and bookings. Kuwait is already fully screened; the same pipeline scales region by region.

Mobile & web

React Native / Expo (SDK 56, New Architecture) for iOS & Android from one codebase; a static web CRM. Distributed via TestFlight and Firebase App Distribution.

Cloud & data

Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging). Google Places (New) + Google Weather APIs. An async planner & AI-screening service.

AI curation

Claude judges every venue once against a family-friendliness rubric (incl. Arabic & "vanity-mislabel" checks); verdicts are cached globally so cost falls as coverage grows.

Engineered to scale

Screen-once-reuse-everywhere caching, role-scoped security rules, and a region-by-region sweep model keep marginal cost low as new markets come online.

Status: pre-launch, in active testing

The product is feature-complete across discovery, planning, memories, commerce and the merchant CRM, shipping to TestFlight + Android testers at build #49. Next: public launch in the GCC, live payments (Stripe), print-album fulfilment partners, and regional catalogue expansion.
