Getting started

Live in one sitting

No setup fee, no long forms. We help you onboard so your first voucher can sell the same day.

1
Claim your place. Find your venue on Spotly (it's likely already listed) and claim it from the partner dashboard or the app.
2
Add your offers. Create prepaid vouchers (e.g. "pay 10 → get 15 balance") and/or enable bookings. We'll set these up with you.
3
Go live & get discovered. Families see your offers in Discover; you can request promoted placement to feature them.
4
Redeem & track. Scan the customer's QR to redeem (app camera or in-browser), and watch your sales, clients & trends in the dashboard.
