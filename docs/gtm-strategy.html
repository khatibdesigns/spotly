The model, precisely

Two layers of supply — and what each earns

Spotly is useful the moment it opens because the catalogue already exists (~2,700 AI-screened kid-friendly places in Kuwait). But it's important to be exact about what makes money: discovery is free and universal; a venue only generates sales once we onboard it and add its vouchers/booking.

Layer 1 — Free discovery (no onboarding)
  • Every AI-screened place appears in Discover, the Map, and the AI planner.
  • Families find, plan, save, visit and remember — full value, zero merchant action.
  • This drives engagement → which powers the revenue that needs no merchant.
Layer 2 — Commerce (must be onboarded)
  • To sell on Spotly, a venue must be claimed/onboarded with vouchers or bookings added.
  • Only these places show "View offers / Book" and generate transaction revenue.
  • So merchant onboarding is the revenue critical path — even though usage isn't gated on it.
Which revenue needs merchants — and which doesn't
Revenue streamNeeds onboarding?Why it matters at launch
Spotly Plus subscriptionNoMonetises every family from day one — independent of any merchant.
Printed albums & printsNoThe marquee earner; needs only family photos, not merchants. De-risked base.
Voucher commerce (take-rate)YesNeeds the venue onboarded + vouchers live. Cash-upfront hook for merchants.
Booking commissionYesNeeds the venue accepting bookings on Spotly.
Promoted placement / merchant SaaSYesSold to onboarded brands; scales with merchant density.

The two consequences that shape the whole plan

1. Because Plus + albums need no merchants, we can launch and earn with families alone — growth is not blocked by the chicken-and-egg.
2. Because transaction revenue needs onboarding, we run a concentrated merchant push for density — onboard the venues families already view most so "buy/book" feels alive, rather than scattering thinly.

Demand side

Acquiring families

GCC parents are mobile-first and high-spend, living on Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat (don't skip Snap in the Gulf). Ranked by leverage:

Built-in loops · cheapest

Virality already in the app

Memory sharing (share a finished album/memory card — every share is an ad parents want to post), family invites (deep links, incentivised), and a referral program (free album mini / KWD off both sides).

Community · best ROI

Where Gulf parents already are

Nurseries, schools, KGs (one director = dozens of families; co-branded weekend guides / class albums), WhatsApp & IG mom-communities, pediatric clinics & kids' salons (QR in waiting rooms).

Creators

Family micro-influencers

10k–100k mom/family creators beat mega-influencers here. Give them a personalised AI plan + a free album of their kids; they post the output, not an ad read.

Paid · after PMF signals

Performance, once retention's proven

Geo-targeted Meta / TikTok / Snap to parents 25–45; Apple Search Ads + Google App campaigns. Creative = the memory book & the AI plan, not feature lists. Keep small until LTV:CAC is understood.

Activation is the job, not just installs

An install only counts once a family completes setup and takes a first action (save a place, generate a plan, add a memory). Optimise the first session for one "wow" — the AI plan or the first memory — and prompt a review right after it.

Supply side

Onboarding establishments (the revenue path)

They're already listed (AI-screened), so this is land-and-expand, not cold seeding. The wedge is their own data: "Families on Spotly are already discovering you — claim your listing free, see who's viewing you, then add offers to convert them." The merchant analytics you built is the sales tool.

Target order — density > breadth
1
Multi-branch family brands (edutainment, indoor play, trampoline parks, family F&B with play areas). One deal = many branches via the brand→country→branch hierarchy. Highest BD leverage.
2
Malls — aggregate dozens of family tenants + double as a marketing channel. A mall partnership = bulk supply.
3
The most-viewed places in the app — use analytics to target the venues families already look at most. Highest-converting onboarding targets; they create commerce density where attention already is.
4
Independents via the self-serve claim flow — scalable, near-zero sales cost.
The sharpest pitch
  • Cash upfront: "Sell prepaid vouchers on Spotly and get paid before the family visits." Cash-flow + new customers is an easy SME yes.
  • Their own traffic: show view/click/booking trends — proof, not promises.
  • Done-for-you onboarding: claim + add vouchers in the first meeting.
Founding Partner program
  • First ~50 venues: free promoted placement 3–6 months + free CRM tier + "Founding Partner" badge.
  • Trade free promotion for a social shout-out → reverse-acquires families.
  • Relationship-led, in-person BD (Gulf B2B isn't won over email).

Why density matters

If only a handful of venues have live vouchers, families rarely hit a "buy" moment and commerce revenue stays flat. Concentrate onboarding so popular areas/categories feel shoppable — then transaction revenue compounds with usage.

Search

SEO & ASO — your most defensible channel

You own a curated dataset competitors don't — that's a moat for both the app stores and the web.

ASO

Rank in the app stores (drives installs)

Bilingual EN + AR listings. Keywords: kids activities Kuwait, things to do with kids, family outings, indoor play, kid-friendly restaurants + Arabic (أماكن عائلية, أنشطة الأطفال, أماكن ترفيهية للأطفال). Outcome-led screenshots. Reviews are the #1 lever — prompt after a great moment.

Programmatic web SEO

Local-intent landing pages at scale

From your data: "Best kid-friendly places in [Area]", "Indoor activities for kids in Kuwait", "Halal family restaurants in [Area]", "Birthday venues in Kuwait" — hundreds of long-tail pages, bilingual, each funnelling into the app.

Content / guides

Evergreen parenting intent

Weekend guides, hot-/rainy-day indoor lists (ties to the weather-aware planner), birthday-party planning, "new to Kuwait with kids." Earns links + ranks long-tail.

Off-page

Local + links

Google Business Profile, structured data on venue pages, backlinks from schools, nurseries, parenting sites, malls & press — sourced via the same partnership outreach.

⚠ Compliance guardrail (build it in from day one)

Google Places terms let you persist only the place_id + your own verdict — not Google's place content (names, photos, reviews). So programmatic pages must be built from Spotly's own curation/descriptions (or live API calls rendered client-side), never republished Google data. This is a feature, not a limit: original, genuinely-helpful curation ranks better and is defensible.

Keep & grow

Retention, lifecycle & metrics

Retention loops (mostly built)
  • Memory loop: more outings logged → stickier + more album revenue. Nudge "add a memory" after a visit.
  • Birthday engine (DOB): the highest-intent moment of a family's year — a push (and later a merchant-funded offer) on the child's birthday. Also a merchant product.
  • Lifecycle push: "this weekend near you," weather-aware ("too hot Saturday — indoor picks"), re-engagement.
  • Plus prompts at value moments (storage cap, full map).
Metrics to instrument now (GA4)
  • North Star: weekly active families taking a meaningful action (save / plan / memory / book).
  • Funnel: install → activate → habit (week-2 return / memory) → monetise → refer.
  • Watch: activation %, D7/D30 retention, K-factor (invites + album shares, aim >0.5), album attach-rate, Plus conversion, merchant claim rate, LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1.

Two revenue engines, two dashboards

Track the merchant-independent engine (Plus + albums per active family) and the merchant-dependent engine (voucher/booking GMV × take-rate, gated on onboarding density) separately. They scale on different inputs — families vs. partner onboarding — and conflating them hides where to push.
