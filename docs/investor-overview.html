The opportunity

A weekly problem for every family

Families everywhere face the same recurring question — "where do we take the kids?" — and the tools they use weren't built for them. Meanwhile, family venues have no efficient way to reach exactly those parents.

Problem
  • Decision fatigue, every weekend. Parents default to the same few places.
  • Generic discovery fails families. Maps surface bars, cafés & adult venues — not age-, diet- or weather-appropriate options.
  • Memories scatter across thousands of camera-roll photos.
  • Local venues can't target parents cost-effectively.
Solution
  • AI-curated discovery — every place vetted for kid-friendliness before it's shown.
  • An AI trip planner tuned to your kids' ages, food & the live weather.
  • A memory map & gallery that converts into printed albums (recurring, high-margin).
  • A merchant platform + CRM for listings, offers, bookings & analytics.

Why now

AI makes it finally possible to curate "family-friendly" at scale and cheaply — a screen-once, reuse-everywhere model. Combined with the GCC's young, mobile-first, high-spend family demographic and an under-served local-experiences market, Spotly can build the default family-outings layer before a generic incumbent does.

Business model

Five reinforcing revenue streams

Spotly monetises both sides of the marketplace. Consumer discovery & bookings stay free to maximise the funnel; revenue comes from subscriptions, transaction take-rates, physical products and merchant services.

①

Spotly Plus subscription

Premium tier (~$4.99/mo, $39.99/yr): unlimited memory storage, full map & advanced AI curation. Recurring consumer revenue.

②

Printed albums & prints

The marquee driver. Custom photo books from a family's gallery — high AOV, high margin, emotionally sticky, repeat-purchase per trip.

③

Bookings & voucher commerce

A take-rate on prepaid vouchers and booking commissions. Free to the family; monetised on the merchant's GMV.

④

Merchant promotion & SaaS

Paid promoted placement + a tiered CRM subscription for multi-branch brands (analytics, team, redemption).

⑤

Data-light partnerships (future)

Sponsored family campaigns, tourism-board & brand collaborations, and white-label family discovery for hotels & malls — incremental, high-margin once the network is dense.

Illustrative unit economics

Subscription

~$40/yr ARPU at 4–8% conversion of active families; near-100% gross margin (digital).

Printed album

~$35 AOV, ~$15–18 contribution after print & ship; recurring per outing/season.

Commerce take

~10% of voucher/booking GMV; scales directly with merchant density & usage.

Strategy

Go-to-market & moat

Go-to-market
  • Land in Kuwait (pre-screened catalogue) → dense local supply + paid & organic family acquisition.
  • Sign anchor brands (multi-branch family venues) via the CRM to seed offers & bookings.
  • Memory loop drives retention — albums & passport keep families returning after each outing.
  • Expand Saudi → Qatar → UAE → EU/US, each launching pre-curated.
Defensibility
  • Compounding curated dataset — screen-once, reuse-everywhere; cost per market falls over time.
  • Two-sided network effects — more families attract venues; more venues enrich discovery.
  • Memory flywheel & switching cost — a family's photo history & map live in Spotly.
  • Localisation moat — bilingual Arabic/RTL, multi-currency, weather-aware: hard for a generic incumbent to retrofit.
Key assumptions behind the model

Acquisition

Blended CAC kept low via organic + referral (family invites) + merchant-driven traffic.

Engagement

Outings are repeat behaviour; the memory loop lifts retention & album attach-rate.

Supply density

Anchor brands + AI base catalogue make each market useful from day one.
